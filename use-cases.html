Webcattus Labs: no inquiry left waiting

A one-person studio where the founder is often on client calls or away from the desk when new inquiries arrive.

Running now

Challenge

  • Inquiries landed in an inbox and waited until the next break

AI solution

Family restaurant: catering calls missed during the dinner rush

A 40-seat restaurant with a growing catering business. Event inquiries come in by phone, mostly between 5 and 9 PM, when nobody can answer.

Illustrative

Challenge

  • About 20 catering and event calls a month go to voicemail
  • Callbacks happen the next day, after many callers have booked elsewhere
  • Staff spend hours a week on phone tag collecting date, headcount and budget

AI solution

  • Instant text-back on every missed call
  • An intake assistant collects event date, guest count, budget and dietary needs by text
  • The manager gets a complete brief with a claim link; an escalation runs if nobody responds in 15 minutes
  • Quote drafts are generated from the catering menu for the manager to approve

Worked example · Home services

HVAC company: after-hours leads and office admin

A six-technician heating and air company. Evening and weekend inquiries pile up, and the office manager spends her mornings turning voicemails into dispatch notes.

Illustrative

Challenge

  • About 40 inquiries a month arrive after hours; only half get a same-day reply
  • Urgent jobs like "no heat" sit in the same voicemail box as routine questions
  • Six hours a week of copying details between voicemail, email and the scheduling app

AI solution

  • Rules spot urgent and safety words and page the on-call tech immediately
  • Routine requests are held and assigned at 8 AM, with a backup and then the owner if nobody claims them
  • AI turns each call and form into a clean job summary in the scheduling app
  • Gas, smoke or sparks always go to a person with a "call now" flag
