About Webcattus Labs

Our Story

Technical depth,
delivered end to end

Webcattus Labs is a boutique data engineering consultancy specializing in cloud-native data solutions for healthcare and enterprise clients. We design, build, and operate modern data platforms that turn raw data into reliable, actionable insights.

Our work spans the full data stack — from ingestion and transformation to orchestration, governance, and analytics delivery. We bring deep expertise in Google Cloud Platform, Apache Spark, Databricks, and Apache Airflow, grounded in both production engineering and academic research.

We operate with a single guiding principle: build systems your team can trust, own, and grow — not black boxes you'll need us to maintain forever.

Ownership over handoffs

We take end-to-end accountability. Gaps in handoffs are where data problems live — we close them.

Research-informed engineering

Peer-reviewed work in data systems and decision intelligence shapes how we design — not just practitioner intuition.

Production-first, always

Day 30 reliability matters more than day 1 demos. We build with monitoring, documentation, and handover in mind from day one.
