Data & privacy

What happens to my business and customer data?+

It stays in your accounts. We connect only the systems a project needs, and only the fields it needs. Customer details aren't copied into spreadsheets or AI prompts unless the task requires it.

Before anything goes live, you get a one-page data map showing what's connected, where data goes and who can see it.

Will my data be used to train AI models?+

We use business and API plans from established AI providers whose terms say your inputs aren't used to train their models. We show you those terms before you approve the build.

Who has access to my systems?+
  • Accounts are created in your name, not ours.
  • We use the narrowest access each task needs, and you can remove it anytime.
  • We never ask you to email passwords. Access is shared through your password manager or each tool's own invite system.
  • When a project ends, our access ends with it unless you're on a retainer.
Can you work with health, legal or financial information?+

Sometimes, with care. If a workflow touches regulated information, such as health records under HIPAA, we only proceed when the tools and agreements it requires are in place. We'll flag it in the audit, and we'll turn the project down rather than cut corners.

Accuracy & control

What if the AI makes something up?+

It's a real risk, so we design for it:

  • Grounded answers: assistants answer only from information you've approved, like your menu, price list and policies.
  • Narrow scope: each agent does one job well instead of trying to answer everything.
  • Handoff when unsure: if a question falls outside what it knows, it says so and passes it to a person.
  • Tested before launch: at least 50 real examples from your business, including tricky ones.
  • Reviewed after launch: conversations are logged and spot-checked every month.
Could it say something embarrassing to a customer?+

Anything sent in your name uses wording you've approved, or goes to a person for approval first. Complaints, refunds, prices and safety issues are always routed to your team.

Can you update Yelp and post review replies automatically?+

Partly. Google Business Profile lets approved tools update hours and post review replies, so those can happen in one tap. Yelp limits what outside tools can change, so for Yelp we prepare the exact update or reply and you post it from your Yelp business account in a few seconds. A weekly check flags any listing that has drifted out of sync.

What isn't AI good at?+

Judgment calls, sensitive conversations, anything where a mistake is expensive, and tasks where the rules change every week. We'll tell you when a task belongs with a person, or with a simple rule instead of AI.

Timeline & cost

How long until something is working?+
  • AI Readiness Audit: 30 minutes, usually within a week of booking.
  • Discovery Workshop: one day, with your report three business days later.
  • Focused agent: live in 2–3 weeks. Connected agent: 3–5 weeks.
What does it cost to run after launch?+

Most builds run for tens of dollars a month in tool and usage fees, paid directly to the providers in your name. You'll see an estimate before you approve anything. Ongoing support is optional and starts at $750 a month.

How will I know it's working?+

We agree on one target number before we start, such as reply time, hours saved or jobs booked. You get a short monthly report against that number, built from the system's own logs.

What if it doesn't hit the target?+

We find out why and fix it. If it still isn't worth running, we'll recommend switching it off. Retainers are month to month, so you're never paying for something that doesn't pay you back.

Working together

Do I need technical staff?+

No. We handle the setup and the technical work. We need someone who knows the workflow for about an hour a week during the build, plus a short training session before launch.

Will AI replace my employees?+

That's not how we design it. The goal is to take the repetitive parts of their jobs, like phone tag, copying details between apps and answering the same question for the tenth time, so your people spend more time on customers and the work only they can do.

Do you only work in Dallas–Fort Worth?+

We're based in Dallas and happy to come on-site anywhere in DFW. Everything else works remotely, anywhere in the U.S.

Which AI tools do you use?+

Whatever fits the job and the tools you already pay for. We're not resellers and don't earn commissions on software, so our recommendations aren't tied to a vendor.
