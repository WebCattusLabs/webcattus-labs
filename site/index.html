The problem

Small businesses don't lose to bigger competitors. They lose to slower ones.

Most small businesses have the same three leaks. None of them need a new hire. All of them can be fixed with well-set-up AI.

The first reply wins.

A customer who can't reach you calls the next business on the list. Calls missed during a rush and forms that sit until morning are revenue walking out the door.

The 9 PM admin shift.

Quotes, follow-ups, scheduling, data entry and the same five customer questions. The owner ends up doing them after closing, every night.

Tools that don't talk.

The phone, the inbox, the booking app and the spreadsheet each hold part of the picture. Things fall through the gaps between them.

How it works

From first call to measurable results in about a month

  1. Find the leak

    Free audit, then week 1

    A 30-minute AI Readiness Audit, then an optional one-day workshop. We map where time and customers slip away and agree on one target number.

  2. Build it with guardrails

    Weeks 2–4

    We set up the automation inside the tools you already use, test it on real examples from your business, and train your team before it goes live.

  3. Measure and improve

    Every month

    A short report on hours saved, replies sent and jobs booked against the target. We tune what's working and switch off what isn't.
