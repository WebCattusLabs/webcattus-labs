The short version

A small studio, on purpose.

Webcattus Labs started from a simple frustration: small businesses deserve a website that works as hard as they do — without the agency-sized invoice or the tech jargon that usually comes with it.

We're founder-led, which means the person who designs your site is the same person who picks up when you call. No account managers, no hand-offs, no "let me loop in the team." Just clear work, done well, by people who actually care whether it lands.

Big enough to build the real thing. Small enough that you're never a ticket number.
