Our AI philosophy

Useful beats impressive.

AI is a tool, not a strategy. These beliefs shape what we recommend, and what we talk clients out of.

Start with the problem, not the technology

We begin with where your week goes and where customers slip away. Sometimes the answer is AI. Sometimes it's a better form or a simple rule, and we'll tell you.

Measure everything

Every project has one target number agreed before we start: hours saved, reply time or revenue recovered. If we can't measure it, we don't sell it.

People stay in charge

AI drafts, sorts and reminds. People approve prices, handle complaints and make judgment calls. Anything safety-related always goes straight to a human.

No lock-in

Everything lives in accounts you own and comes with documentation. You should keep working with us because it pays off, not because you're stuck.

Who we work with

Owner-led businesses with 2 to 50 people

Especially restaurants, home-services companies and professional practices across Dallas–Fort Worth. We work remotely anywhere in the U.S.
